# BlankDelay Affiliate Program — Owner Setup

## What you get
- **Owner dashboard:** https://blankdelay.com/affiliate-owner
- See every affiliate email, code, clicks, sales, products sold
- Their earnings (**80%** of attributed sales) and **your 20%** share
- Cash-out queue: when they withdraw, **another 20%** of that withdrawal is your fee; pay them the **net** via PayPal and mark paid
- Affiliates: signup/login, live click tracking, Stripe sale attribution, forgot-password reset

## Required Netlify env var
Set this in Netlify → Site settings → Environment variables:

```
AFFILIATE_ADMIN_TOKEN=pick-a-long-random-secret-only-you-know
```

Optional overrides:
```
AFFILIATE_COMMISSION_RATE=0.8
OWNER_SALE_SHARE=0.2
CASHOUT_FEE_RATE=0.2
```

Redeploy after setting the token.

## Passwords (important)
Affiliate passwords are **hashed** and **never shown** in the owner panel (including for people who already applied).  
If someone forgets theirs: they use **Forgot password**, or you click **Reset link** on their row and send them the link.

When you **Create** an affiliate in the owner panel, a temporary password is shown **once** so you can text it to them.

## Existing applicants
Old browser-only signups were stored only on their device. Have them sign up again on the live site, or create their account in the owner panel with their email.

## How tracking works
1. Visitor opens `https://blankdelay.com/blankdelayaffiliatetweaks?aff=AFF-XXXXXX`
2. Click is recorded on the server
3. Checkout Stripe links include `client_reference_id=AFF-XXXXXX`
4. Stripe webhook credits the affiliate (80%) and your share (20%)

## Cash-out flow
1. Affiliate clicks **Cash Out** (min $5)
2. System deducts 20% as your fee and creates a pending payout for the remaining 80%
3. You pay their PayPal the **net** amount from the owner dashboard
4. Click **Mark paid**
