Last updated: July 9, 2026

BlankDelay ("we," "us," or "our") operates blankdelay.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Our site may use cookies or local storage for preferences (such as theme settings), cart state, and basic functionality. We do not use cookies for invasive cross-site tracking.

We retain order and license records as long as needed to provide support, honor refunds, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of non-essential personal data by contacting us.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, secure payment processing, and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe we have done so.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Email blankdelayy@gmail.com to make a request.
